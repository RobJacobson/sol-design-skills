Build a web app called Streakly, a habit tracker for people who want to build small daily habits.

1. Landing page: a hero section with a headline and a sign-up button, three feature highlights, a pricing section with a Free plan and a Pro plan, and a footer.
2. Dashboard: today's habits as a checklist, a current streak counter, a weekly completion chart, and a 12-week completion grid.
3. Add habit flow: a simple form with habit name, icon, and how many days per week.
4. Settings page: name, email, a notifications toggle, and a dark mode toggle.

Engineering requirements: use React with Tailwind. Use realistic sample data so every screen looks populated. Make every page fully responsive on mobile and desktop.



Work autonomously. Test the app yourself before declaring completion. Do not stop after creating a mockup or partial prototype.
